Exclusions and Limitations

PPO Dentist, Premier Dentist
Description Exclusions and Limitations
Diagnostic

Oral examinations (including examinations by a specialist) are payable twice per calendar year.
Preventitive

Prophylaxes (cleanings) are payable twice per calendar year.

Two periodontal prophylaxes are payable per calendar year for individuals with a document history of periodontal disease.

Fluoride treatements are payable once per calendar year for age 18 and under.

One additional fluoride is payable for members with moderate to high risk of caries.

Space maintains are payable once per area per 60 consecutive months, for missing posterior primary teeth, for age 13 and under.
Bitewing Radiographs

Bitewing x-rays are payable without a time limitation.
All Other Radiographs

Full mouth x-rays (which include bitewing x-rays) or a panorex are payable once in a five-year period.

Sealants

Sealants are payable for first and second permanent molars for age 15 and under, limited to once per tooth per lifetime.

Minor Restorative

Amalgam and composite resin restorations are payable once within in a 24-month period, regardless of the number of combination of restorations placed on a surface.

Composite resin (white) restorations are not covered on molar teeth.
Major Restorative

Cast restorations (including crowns and onlays) and associated procedures (such as cores and substructures) on the same tooth are payable once in any five-year period.

Three or more surface inlays are covered benefits.

Posterior porcelain crowns are covered benefits.
Periodontics

Occlusal guards are not covered by the dental plan.

Root planing and scaling is payable once per quadrant in 24 consecutive months.
Other Basic Services

Diagnostic photographs and cephalometric films, unless done for covered orthodontics, are not benefits of the dental plan.

Diagnostic casts, unless done for covered othodontics, are not benefits of the dental plans.
Prosthodontics

Payment will be made to replace a tooth that has been missing prior to the effective date of coverage.

Posterior porcelain or resin fused to metal (facings) and all porcelain ceramic for fixed prosthodontics are covered benefits.

Bridgework is limited to once in a five year period.

Full and partial dentures are limited to once in a five year period.
Implants

Implants are payable once per tooth per life time. Implant supported restorations are payable once per tooth per five years.
